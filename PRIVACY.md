# Disk Survey Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026. This policy applies to the Mac App Store edition of Disk Survey.

## Summary

Disk Survey does not collect, transmit or share any data. It makes no network connections. Everything it does happens on your Mac.

## What the app reads

To show what fills a disk, Disk Survey reads the names, sizes and dates of the files and folders in the location you choose. It does not read the contents of your files.

The app can read only the disks and folders you choose in the system's open panel. If you additionally switch on Full Disk Access in System Settings, which is optional, it can also count folders that macOS protects separately, such as Mail, Messages, Safari data and the Trash. You can switch this off again at any time in System Settings.

When it shows the helper panel for Full Disk Access, the app reads the position of the System Settings window on screen so the panel can be placed next to it. Nothing else about other apps is read.

Scan results are kept in memory while a window is open and are discarded when you close it or quit the app.

## What is stored on your Mac

Disk Survey stores the following in its own private container on your Mac, and nowhere else:

- your settings,
- the list of locations you scanned recently,
- access bookmarks, which let the app reopen a disk or folder you chose without asking again.

You can remove stored locations in Settings under Scanning. Deleting Disk Survey and its data from your Mac removes all of the above.

## What is not collected

- No personal information
- No analytics, usage statistics or crash reports collected by the app
- No advertising or tracking identifiers
- No accounts or sign-in
- No third-party code or services

If you have chosen in macOS to share diagnostics with app developers, Apple may provide anonymized crash and usage data for the App Store edition. That is collected by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, not by Disk Survey, and you can turn it off in System Settings under Privacy & Security, Analytics & Improvements.

## Changes to your files

Disk Survey changes nothing on your disk unless you ask it to. When you choose Move to Trash and confirm, the selected item is moved to the Trash, where it stays until you put it back or empty the Trash.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
